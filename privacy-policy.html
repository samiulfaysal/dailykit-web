Privacy Policy — DailyKit

Package: com.nlab.dailykit • Developer: NLab • Effective Date: November 2026

NLab built DailyKit as an offline-first utility application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

1. Local On-Device Processing

All calculations, unit conversions, saved preferences, and utility entries are processed and stored locally on your device. DailyKit does not require account registration and does not transmit your personal entries to any external server.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free, DailyKit displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, crash logs, diagnostics, and ad interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera (Optional, if QR/Barcode Scanner is used): Used solely on-device to scan QR codes or barcodes when you explicitly open the scanner tool. No camera images or video are recorded or uploaded.
  • Internet & Network State: Used to load AdMob advertisements and open external web links (such as Privacy Policy or Play Store rating).

4. Children's Privacy

DailyKit is a general-purpose utility application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or DailyKit, contact us at support@nlab.click or visit https://dailykit.nlab.click.
